This section collects the ten numbered formulas used in the pipeline and analyses; each is referenced by number where it is used.

(1) Locus clustering. HSPs on one contig are sorted by genomic start and chained while the gap to the running locus end stays within the frozen span: locus = { h_i : s_{i+1} - max_end(h_1..h_i) <= 50 kb }. A cluster becomes one candidate locus with coordinates [min start, max end].

(2) Total locus bits. bits(locus) = sum_i bits(h_i), the sum of bitscores of all HSPs assigned to the locus.

(3) Strong-locus criterion. A locus is strong iff bits(locus) >= 100. Presence of a gene in a genome is defined as at least one strong locus; the copy signal is the number of strong loci.

(4) Query coverage. qcov(locus) = max over HSPs of (q_end - q_start + 1) / q_len, the largest fraction of the query protein covered by a single HSP in the locus, used as the domain-integrity proxy.

(5) Shuffled-control false-positive rate. FPR(genome) = (number of shuffled queries yielding at least one strong locus) / (number of shuffled queries), with the pre-registered validity criterion FPR <= 0.05.

(6) Binomial mapping test (H1, H2). For k mapped genes of n panel genes with control rate p0, p = P(X >= k) for X ~ Binomial(n, p0), with p0 = max(FPR, 1/126), the conservative upper bound from 125 shuffled queries.

(7) Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment. For m p-values sorted p_(1) <= ... <= p_(m), q_(i) = min over j>=i of (m/j) * p_(j), applied per hypothesis family at the frozen 0.05 level.

(8) Copy-share Fisher contrast (H3). For gene g with strong-locus counts c_Td and c_ot and group totals T_Td, T_ot, the 2x2 table [[c_Td, c_ot], [T_Td - c_Td, T_ot - c_ot]] is tested by two-sided Fisher exact, with BH across genes.

(9) Jaccard distance and separation statistic (H4). For presence sets A, B: d_J(A,B) = 1 - |A intersect B| / |A union B|; the separation statistic is mean d_J within the T. dohrnii assemblies minus mean d_J between T. dohrnii and the non-reverting genomes, tested exactly over all 6 label assignments.

(10) Assembly-aware evolutionary evidence score. AEES = 0.35 * min(bits/500, 1) + 0.25 * qcov + 0.15 * pident + 0.25 * concordance, where concordance in {0, 0.5, 1} counts detection in neither, one, or both T. dohrnii assemblies; for single-assembly genomes the first three weights renormalize by 1/0.75. Confidence bands: high >= 0.75, moderate >= 0.45, low below.

(11) Recall (benchmark arms). recall = (genes with at least one strong locus under the arm) / (panel size), compared paired: multi-species versus human-only queries on Clytia, and genome-first versus database-first on T. dohrnii.

Formulas 1-3 and 5-6 are pre-registered and frozen; formula 8 implements the pre-registered Fisher contrast and its exact table is documented in docs/X-DEVIATIONS.md (D2); formula 10 is the judge-round R1 addition and is exploratory; formula 11 is the benchmark measurement. The numbering is used throughout the results chapters.
