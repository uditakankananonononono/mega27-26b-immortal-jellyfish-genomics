The assembly-aware evolutionary evidence score (AEES) was introduced to convert raw similarity calls into confidence-graded evolutionary evidence (method added in response to adversarial review, logged in docs/X-JUDGE-ROUNDS.md round R1; the layer is additive and does not alter the pre-registered presence and copy calls). For each gene in each genome, AEES combines four components with weights frozen before outcomes were read: the normalized total bitscore of the best locus (weight 0.35), the query coverage of that locus as a domain-integrity proxy (0.25), the best percent identity (0.15), and, for the two T. dohrnii assemblies only, a concordance term that scores 1.0 when a gene is detected in both assemblies, 0.5 in one, and 0.0 in neither (0.25). For single-assembly genomes the concordance weight redistributes proportionally over the remaining components. Genes with no strong locus are labeled not detected rather than absent, because a missing hit on a draft assembly cannot distinguish true loss from an assembly gap.

Applying the frozen high-confidence cutoff (AEES >= 0.75) yields 20 high-confidence genes in the main T. dohrnii assembly and 17 in the fragmented Oviedo assembly, against 3 in T. rubra and 2 in A. aurita, with the annotated Clytia calibration at 8. The high-confidence T. dohrnii set, with scores, is: ATG5 (0.86), APP (0.83), ATG7 (0.82), PSEN1 (0.81), GHR (0.80), SIRT6 (0.79), SIRT1 (0.79), GRN (0.79), VCP (0.78), TARDBP (0.78), HTT (0.78), LRRK2 (0.77), ATR (0.77), INSR (0.77), TFEB (0.77), BLM (0.77), SQSTM1 (0.77), C9orf72 (0.76), BECN1 (0.76), MTOR (0.76). The contrast between the two mortal relatives (2-3 high-confidence genes) and the two immortal-lineage assemblies (17-20) is descriptive at this stage; the formal tests are H1-H4, and the per-gene scores are reported in full in results/x-aees.json. The concordance component also quantifies the assembly-quality problem directly: genes detected in only one T. dohrnii assembly carry concordance 0.5 and visibly lower scores, which is exactly the uncertainty the second assembly was kept to measure.

AEES serves two functions in this project. Internally, it is the confidence rubric for every downstream claim: any gene discussed as a candidate carries its score and its concordance status. Externally, it is a methodological contribution for comparative genomics on fragmented non-model genomes, where the standard output (hit found, hit not found) throws away exactly the evidence gradient that distinguishes a solid call from an assembly artifact. The score's limits are stated plainly: it has no expression component because no T. dohrnii transcriptome was used in the frozen design, and its phylogenetic component is reduced to percent identity pending a tree-based extension; both extensions are registered as future arms rather than claimed here.
