# External aging-database annotation of the 48-gene panel

To place the panel in the landscape of existing aging and longevity research, we annotated every panel gene against seven external resources in live queries executed for this expansion (registry and raw outputs: docs/X-TOOLS.md, results/xext/). The results quantify something the panel was designed to do: it is not an arbitrary gene list but a dense intersection of the strongest aging-relevant annotations available.

GenAge, the reference database of genes associated with aging in humans, contains 29 of the 48 panel genes (60.4%), including AKT1, APOE, APP, ATM, ATR, BDNF, BLM, CDKN2A, FOXO3, GHR, GRN, HSF1, HTT, IGF1, IGF1R, INSR, MAPT, MTOR, NFE2L2, NGF, PSEN1, PTEN, SIRT1, SIRT6, SOD1, SQSTM1, TP53, VCP and WRN. The GenAge model-organism compendium (curated lifespan-modifying genes in yeast, worm, fly and mouse) contains 20 panel genes, among them ATG5 and ATG7 - direct experimental evidence that perturbing these autophagy genes changes lifespan in multiple model systems. CellAge, the database of genes driving cellular senescence, contains 21 panel genes including ATG5, ATG7 and BECN1, the three autophagy-initiation genes at the center of our discovery candidate. LongevityMap, the registry of human genetic association studies of longevity, contains 22 panel genes.

Pathway-level annotation agrees. The KEGG longevity-regulating pathways (hsa04211 and hsa04213) contain seven panel genes: FOXO3, IGF1, IGF1R, INSR, MTOR, PRKAA1 and SIRT1 - the insulin/IGF-1-mTOR-FOXO axis that is the most replicated longevity module in biology. The KEGG autophagy pathway (hsa04140) contains seven more: ATG7, C9orf72, IGF1R, MTOR, OPTN, PRKAA1 and SQSTM1. All 47 unique panel symbols resolve to a reviewed human UniProt entry (47/47).

The mirror image of this density is the jellyfish literature desert. Querying PubMed for "Turritopsis dohrnii" returns 14 publications in total, and intersecting the species with ATG5, TERT or SIRT1 returns zero papers each (results/xext/x-ext-pubmed-literature.json). Europe PMC, with broader indexing, returns 60 publications for the species but only 1 for ATG5, 1 for ATG7 and 0 for BECN1 (results/xext/x-ext-europepmc-literature.json). The genes that aging research has spent decades validating have, with single-paper exceptions, never been examined in the one animal that reverses its life cycle. That asymmetry - maximal external validation of the targets, minimal examination in the organism - is exactly the gap this project works in.

For the lead discovery candidate specifically, the external resources corroborate identity and context: human ATG5 is the reviewed UniProt entry Q9H1Y0 with a deposited AlphaFold model, a defined domain architecture in InterPro, nine associated PDB structures, four Reactome pathway annotations centered on autophagy, and a high-confidence STRING interaction module connecting ATG5, ATG7, BECN1 and SQSTM1 - the same module whose members show elevated AEES in T. dohrnii in our mapping (section 08c). These records establish that the human query protein we mapped from is the canonical autophagy factor, and that its interactors are exactly the genes our scoring flags as jellyfish-enriched.
