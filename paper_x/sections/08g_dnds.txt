# Selection analysis: dN/dS between T. dohrnii and its mortal sister (exploratory)

Judge weakness 3 of the provided round asked for evolutionary convergence analysis. This section reports a first selection analysis, labeled exploratory because it was not part of the pre-registered hypothesis set and because it uses partial-gene spans rather than full coding sequences. For each of the ten top-AEES genes of the pre-repair ranking plus the repair-affected cases (ATG5, APP, ATG7, PSEN1, GHR, SIRT6, SIRT1, GRN, VCP, TARDBP), we extracted the strongest tBLASTn hit span from the primary T. dohrnii assembly and from T. rubra, translated each span in the correct reading frame, aligned the two proteins with MAFFT 7.525, imposed the alignment on the nucleotide sequences to build a codon alignment, and computed pairwise dN/dS under the Nei-Gojobori 1986 method (experiments/x_dnds.py, results/x-dnds.json).

After the D9 query-integrity repair, seven of the ten genes yield an estimable synonymous rate, and all seven show omega below 1: ATG5 at 0.030, ATG7 at 0.066, SIRT6 at 0.147, GRN at 0.350, TARDBP at 0.434, VCP at 0.437, and PSEN1 at 0.835. Two (APP, SIRT1) return saturated synonymous divergence under NG86 and yield no usable estimate, and GHR has no T. dohrnii locus at all under its verified human query - its pre-repair estimate belonged to a mislabeled protein and is withdrawn. The direction of the result is consistent: the aging and longevity loci shared by the two Turritopsis species are under purifying selection, with no sign of relaxed constraint and no sign of positive selection at the whole-locus scale in the immortal lineage. Whatever distinguishes T. dohrnii is not sequence decay or accelerated protein change in these genes.

Two readings deserve emphasis. First, for ATG5 specifically, strong purifying selection on the strongest locus (omega 0.030) shows the autophagy locus is being maintained as a functional gene rather than eroding into a pseudogene. Whether the second cnidarian-type copy in the main assembly is a true duplication kept under the same constraint or an assembly haplotig is the open question of section 08d; the omega estimate here uses the single strongest locus and does not depend on the answer. Second, PSEN1 at 0.835 sits near neutrality, but its estimate rests on only 102 codons, and we report it without interpretation rather than inflate a short-span number. The upgrade path is clear and is logged as future work: full-length coding sequences, more taxa, and a branch model (PAML) that can test the T. dohrnii branch specifically for episodic selection, which a pairwise NG86 comparison cannot detect.
