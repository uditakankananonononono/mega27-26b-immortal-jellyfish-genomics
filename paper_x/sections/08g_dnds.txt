# Selection analysis: dN/dS between T. dohrnii and its mortal sister (exploratory)

Judge weakness 3 of the provided round asked for evolutionary convergence analysis. This section reports a first selection analysis, labeled exploratory because it was not part of the pre-registered hypothesis set and because it uses partial-gene spans rather than full coding sequences. For each of the ten top-AEES genes (ATG5, APP, ATG7, PSEN1, GHR, SIRT6, SIRT1, GRN, VCP, TARDBP), we extracted the strongest tBLASTn hit span from the primary T. dohrnii assembly and from T. rubra, translated each span in the correct reading frame, aligned the two proteins with MAFFT 7.525, imposed the alignment on the nucleotide sequences to build a codon alignment, and computed pairwise dN/dS under the Nei-Gojobori 1986 method (experiments/x_dnds.py, results/x-dnds.json).

Six of the ten genes yield an estimable synonymous rate, and all six show omega below 1: ATG7 at 0.066 over 112 codons, ATG5 at 0.124 over 179 codons, SIRT6 at 0.147 over 420 codons, GRN at 0.350 over 218 codons, TARDBP at 0.434 over 161 codons, and PSEN1 at 0.835 over 102 codons. The remaining four (APP, GHR, SIRT1, VCP) return saturated synonymous divergence under NG86 and yield no usable estimate. The direction of the result is consistent: the aging and longevity loci shared by the two Turritopsis species are under purifying selection, with no sign of relaxed constraint and no sign of positive selection at the whole-locus scale in the immortal lineage. Whatever distinguishes T. dohrnii is not sequence decay or accelerated protein change in these genes.

Two readings deserve emphasis. First, for ATG5 specifically, strong purifying selection on the strongest locus (omega 0.124) is evidence that the duplicated autophagy loci are being maintained as functional copies rather than eroding into pseudogenes - the duplication reported in section 08d is kept under constraint. Second, PSEN1 at 0.835 sits near neutrality, but its estimate rests on only 102 codons, and we report it without interpretation rather than inflate a short-span number. The upgrade path is clear and is logged as future work: full-length coding sequences, more taxa, and a branch model (PAML) that can test the T. dohrnii branch specifically for episodic selection, which a pairwise NG86 comparison cannot detect.
