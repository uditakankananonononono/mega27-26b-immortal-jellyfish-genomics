# Evidence tiers for the candidate set

Judge weakness 19 asked that every candidate carry an explicit evidence class rather than a single undifferentiated confidence claim. We classify each candidate into four tiers. Tier 1 is a genomic locus: the gene maps to a strong locus in the T. dohrnii main assembly; assembly concordance is recorded separately. Tier 2 is orthology-confirmed: the reciprocal-best-hit control retrieves the intended gene as the top reverse hit, supporting orthology for the tested best loci, though this alone does not rule out every paralog or validate an extra copy. Tier 3 is expression-supported: a locus has adequately powered, replicate-aware evidence of expression change across reversal. This is a separate evidence dimension, not a prerequisite for tier 4. Tier 4 is function-annotated: the gene carries independent membership in an external aging or longevity resource (GenAge human or model organisms, CellAge, LongevityMap, or the KEGG longevity and autophagy pathways).

Applied to the current candidate set, the tiers look like this. All 35 panel genes with strong T. dohrnii loci sit at tier 1. The ten top-AEES candidates all reach tier 2, with twenty of twenty reciprocal-best-hit tests passing across the two assemblies (results/x-rbh.json). Nine of those ten have tier-4 annotation: SOD1 (all four databases), ATG5 (GenAge models, CellAge), ATG7 (GenAge models, CellAge, KEGG autophagy), SIRT1 (GenAge human and models, CellAge, LongevityMap, KEGG longevity), SIRT6 (all four databases), APP (GenAge human), PSEN1 (GenAge human, LongevityMap), GRN (GenAge human and models, CellAge), and VCP (GenAge human). TFEB stops at tier 2: it is a strong, orthology-confirmed locus with no independent aging-database membership in our annotation set, and the paper claims nothing more for it. GHR, a pre-repair candidate, was withdrawn entirely after the D9 repair showed its loci belonged to a mislabeled query protein. Tier 3 remains empty: the exploratory 1% RNA-seq sample was completed, but ATG5 cells had fewer than ten read ends and each stage has one sampled library. Neither it nor other targets qualify as replicate-aware differential expression (section 08j).

The tier system also disciplines the discovery claim. ATG5, the paper's named candidate, has tier-4 external annotation alongside orthology-supported ATG5-matching sequence (the extra copy remains unconfirmed) and external autophagy-pathway annotation, but it has no tier-3 support. Every figure and statement about ATG5 in this paper should be read against that classification: a genomic homology and external-annotation candidate, with strong expression-change evidence still pending.
