# Evidence tiers for the candidate set

Judge weakness 19 asked that every candidate carry an explicit evidence class rather than a single undifferentiated confidence claim. We classify each candidate into four tiers. Tier 1 is a genomic locus: the gene maps to a strong locus in both independent T. dohrnii assemblies. Tier 2 is orthology-confirmed: the reciprocal-best-hit control retrieves the intended gene as the top reverse hit, ruling out paralogous cross-matching. Tier 3 is expression-supported: the locus changes expression across the medusa-to-polyp reversal in transcriptomic data. Tier 4 is function-annotated: the gene carries independent membership in an external aging or longevity resource (GenAge human or model organisms, CellAge, LongevityMap, or the KEGG longevity and autophagy pathways).

Applied to the current candidate set, the tiers look like this. All 38 panel genes with strong T. dohrnii loci sit at tier 1. The ten top-AEES candidates all reach tier 2, with twenty of twenty reciprocal-best-hit tests passing across the two assemblies (results/x-rbh.json). Nine of those ten reach tier 4: ATG5 (GenAge models, CellAge), ATG7 (GenAge models, CellAge, KEGG autophagy), SIRT1 (GenAge human and models, CellAge, LongevityMap, KEGG longevity), SIRT6 (all four databases), APP (GenAge human), PSEN1 (GenAge human, LongevityMap), GHR (GenAge human and models, LongevityMap), GRN (GenAge human and models, CellAge), and VCP (GenAge human). TARDBP stops at tier 2: it is a strong, orthology-confirmed locus with no independent aging-database membership, and the paper claims nothing more for it. Tier 3 is empty for every candidate, because the reversal RNA-seq quantification (amendment 6 of the provided round) is still in progress; no candidate is claimed as expression-supported, and the tier structure makes that gap visible instead of hiding it.

The tier system also disciplines the discovery claim. ATG5, the paper's named candidate, stands at tier 4 with orthology-confirmed duplicated loci and external autophagy-pathway annotation, but it does not yet stand at tier 3. Every figure and statement about ATG5 in this paper should be read against that classification: a genomic and functional-annotation discovery, with expression evidence explicitly pending.
