# The unbiased genome-wide arm: all of GenAge through the same pipeline

Judge weakness 16 asked for a panel-free discovery arm: if the pipeline only ever sees the genes we chose to look for, every enrichment claim is circular. We therefore fetched the complete GenAge human compendium - 307 genes, of which 302 were retrieved and exact-gene verified through the same UniProt route as the repaired panel (five had no reviewed human match and are disclosed in data/xpanel_genage/verify_report.json) - and mapped all 302 through the frozen pipeline on the T. dohrnii main assembly (experiments/x_map.py genage mode, results/x-ledger-genage.json). Two hundred thirty-one genes produce ledger entries, 188 with at least one strong locus, and every gene was scored with the same frozen AEES weights, with the concordance component initially held at its single-assembly value pending the Oviedo rerun (experiments/x_genage_rank.py, results/x-genage-aees.json).

In the initial main-assembly-only phase, the first result was an honesty check on the score itself. At that phase the top of the genome-wide ranking was dominated by ancient, ubiquitously conserved machinery - ubiquitin B (0.875), RAD51 (0.846), HDAC3 (0.817), HSPA8 (0.809), EEF1A1, HSP90AA1, MT-CO1 - exactly the genes any BLAST-evidence score should rank highest in any animal genome, because their long, slowly evolving proteins produce the strongest alignments. AEES behaves as designed, and that behavior is why the curated panel is not enriched at the top of the whole-GenAge ranking (Mann-Whitney p = 0.35; five panel genes in the top sixty). We report this plainly: the panel's value was never that its members out-align housekeeping genes, but that it connects the longevity and neurology literature; the genome-wide arm confirms the score measures conservation evidence, not panel membership.

The second initial-phase result was the discovery yield the amendment asked for. Ranking high among GenAge genes the panel never included at that phase were RAD51 (0.846), the homologous-recombination repair factor; HDAC3 (0.817) and HDAC1/HDAC2, the chromatin-maintenance deacetylases; CHEK2 (0.71), the DNA-damage checkpoint kinase; SERPINE1 (0.727), the canonical senescence marker PAI-1; and SOD2 (0.68), the mitochondrial superoxide dismutase - which pairs with the panel's rank-one candidate SOD1 to put both cellular superoxide dismutases among the highest-evidence loci in the immortal jellyfish. These were not panel-chosen narratives; they are what a broader GenAge sweep of the aging-gene universe returns when the frozen pipeline is pointed at T. dohrnii, and they converge on the same modules the panel analysis flagged: genome maintenance, chromatin control, and redox defense. The second-assembly and congener checks below strengthen locus-level detection; expression and function remain untested, so no genome-wide candidate is claimed as a mechanism of rejuvenation.

The second-assembly concordance check specified in the amendment is now complete. We mapped the same 302 verified GenAge query proteins to the independent Oviedo T. dohrnii assembly and T. rubra with the unchanged tBLASTn e-value (1e-5), minimum strong-locus score (100 bits), and locus-clustering rule. Both T. dohrnii assemblies have 231 genes with a hit and 188 with a strong locus; 184 strong calls overlap, four are main-only (COQ7, FEN1, NFE2L2, TCF3), and four are Oviedo-only (CISD2, ERCC8, NOG, PPARG). T. rubra has 233 genes with a hit and 192 with a strong locus. Of the 184 shared T. dohrnii strong calls, 177 are also strong in T. rubra; the seven with no strong T. rubra call (GDF11, HBP1, LMNB1, POLB, PTEN, PTGS2, TPP2) are absence-of-detection observations, not T. dohrnii-specific genes. Assembly quality and phylogenetic distance confound absence calls. This congener result does not establish a lifespan association.

Recomputed AEES with the frozen two-assembly concordance weight raises the top conserved genes by 0.125 for agreement: UBB 1.000, RAD51 0.971, HDAC3 0.942 and HSPA8 0.934. These are homology/evidence scores, not functional-effect estimates. Five curated-panel genes remain in the top sixty; the one-sided Mann-Whitney comparison still does not support curated-panel enrichment (p=0.356, versus 0.348 in the single-assembly phase). The Oviedo and T. rubra result files and the frozen-scoring rerun are in results/x-ledger-genage.json, results/x-tblastn-genage.json and results/x-genage-aees.json.
