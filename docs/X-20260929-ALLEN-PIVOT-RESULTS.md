# Allen plasma-proteomics perturbation-transfer test: measured result, not novel biomarkers

Run on September 29, 2026 after the design lock at commit `1166e16`. Source data and identities in `data/allen-aging/metadata_audit.json`; analysis scripts and numeric JSON in that directory. Public data come from https://apps.allenimmunology.org/aifi/insights/dynamics-imm-health-age/downloads/olink/ . No jellyfish outcome is added by this human-cohort analysis.

**Source and audit.** Sound Life Olink CSV 289,393,778 bytes (SHA256 `2fd48dee27eb72733143abf5e3b76196362f9ca4920211261763178bcb7c9bd0`), 96 donor GUIDs and 867 sample kits; 92 have both Year-1 flu Day 0 and Day 7. Older-cohort CSV 127,288,549 bytes (SHA256 `bf03c0decad33f8221d4940ac6abe443db23814494629c5b496b91018ae3fe7f`), 229 plasma donors with one kit each. Assays joined by stable Olink assay ID. Because 133,991 Sound Life and 65,649 older-cohort NPX rows fall below the matched normalized limit of detection, they were set missing; blank NPX added another 523 and 15,801 rows. One selected external value required the Sound Life training median. A baseline sample never crosses donor-fold boundaries. Nine externally reported ages `89+` were not converted into exact ages and are outside numeric-age regression.

**Internal locked test.** In 92 baseline/day-7 paired donors, 47 younger and 45 older, five donor-blocked hash folds each selected at most five assays using training-only covariate adjustment and the frozen stability rules. Between 123 and 155 eligible stable assays remained in each fold. Held-out Day-0 AUROC 0.9806 (2,000 donor-bootstrap 95% CI 0.9476-1.000); Day-7 AUROC 0.9863, difference +0.0057 (paired CI -0.0068 to +0.0228). Corrected end-to-end age-label permutations, stratified by sex and CMV, were recomputed after fixing an early null-only label-leak bug; **the first 47 invalid null results were discarded**. None of 1,000 valid null runs reached the observed baseline AUROC (max 0.7560), yielding plus-one p=0.000999. All gates passed. This is an easy binary discrimination task because the younger and older Sound Life groups are separated by a roughly twenty-year unsampled age gap; it is not an individual age clock.

**External transfer.** The final five assay IDs, in fixed Sound Life rank order: OID20622/CXCL17; OID20254/LTBP2; OID21451/EDA2R; OID20460/WNT9A; OID21048/SCARF2. Across 220 older-cohort donors with numeric ages (40-89), the frozen continuous score has partial rank association r=0.7853 with age after adjusting for sex, CMV and three Olink batches; prespecified one-sided positive p=9.64e-47. Each of five assay-wise adjusted age-rank signs agrees with its development direction, and all five BH-adjusted q values are <0.05 (range ~8.1e-38 to 3.32e-14); the five exact numbers are in `data/allen-aging/external_verdict.json`. An unadjusted Spearman correlation of 0.7685 is descriptive. This demonstrates a strong transfer of an age-associated signal, NOT calibration of individual age, assay interchangeability, clinical utility, disease prediction, or a causal aging mechanism.

**Prior art and novelty verdict.** The Allen study already found 69 differential age proteins and plotted CXCL17 and WNT9A by age and visit: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09686-5 and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11419011/ . A published ProtAge20 explicitly includes CXCL17, LTBP2 and SCARF2: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11405266/ . An independent frailty/mortality panel includes EDA2R, LTBP2 and WNT9A: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11979046/ . Longitudinal protein stability itself was tested in thousands of adults: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-72957-w . **All five proteins are known age- or frailty-related candidates. This test is a useful independent perturbation-robustness exercise, not a newly discovered aging biomarker signature.** The literature search did not establish whether this exact five-assay rule was published; absence of a search hit is not novelty. Do not submit a paper presenting the protein identities as new.

**Implementation limitations to audit next.** Age-band target was reconstructed from exact numeric ages (threshold 50) because the compact matrix metadata did not carry `subject.ageGroup`; the source cohort's two non-overlapping 25-35 and 55-65 groups make this equivalent, but verify against source labels before a formal paper. The locked plan mentioned a sex/CMV-only and full-assay comparator and an unstable five-assay comparator; those descriptive comparators have not yet been run. The final selected assays were selected by training-only univariate residualized score and ridge logistic fit; this does not establish independent contribution of all five. Cross-cohort Olink 1536/3072 NPX scale may have batch/platform shifts; only association direction was tested, not absolute calibration. The permutation code requires an honest re-check of whether conditional stability filters are label-aware; its corrected code now is, and first four cached runs exactly matched uncached recomputation. This is a measured exploratory report with clear boundaries, not a clinical result.
