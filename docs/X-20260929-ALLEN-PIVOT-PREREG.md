## Why this narrow question

The original Nature study already found 69 baseline age-differential plasma proteins and followed selected markers across visits; it also profiled the older cohort. Broad age-protein discovery is prior art. Other work has studied longitudinal protein stability and proteomic age scores. The distinct, testable narrower question is whether a **very small score trained on Sound Life age groups remains age-discriminating after within-person vaccination perturbation and transfers to the independent older-cohort assay**. This is a dataset-specific validation attempt, not an assertion of novel biology or clinical utility. Source links in `X-20260929-ALLEN-PIVOT-SOURCE-AUDIT.md`.

## Locked Sound Life development and internal evaluation

Use one `Flu Year 1 Day 0` baseline sample per donor (92), its `Flu Year 1 Day 7` mate (92), and where available `Flu Year 2 Day 0` (84) and `Flu Year 2 Day 7` (83). Subject age group (`Young Adult` vs `Older Adult`) is the binary target; chronological age is NOT the first-stage target because its training ages have a 35-55-year gap. `subject.subjectGuid` determines donor-blocked folds, and the first five folds are SHA256(subjectGuid + literal salt `allen-pivot-20260929-v1`) mod 5. If one class has fewer than five donors in any fold, replace with deterministic donor-stratified balanced fivefold allocation sorted by hash, log the switch before outcome fitting. All assays must be measured in at least 90% of baseline samples and above paired LOD in at least 75% of baseline samples *in each age group* within each training fold; all filtering and median/std normalization uses training folds only. Candidate assays are those shared by both cohorts and identified by stable assay ID. Fit sex + CMV covariates and the age-group signal as a nested development model; age-group target must not leak from normalization. A model using all 1,472 assays and a sex/CMV-only model are comparators.

For each outer fold, select at most FIVE assays with largest absolute training-only residualized standardized age-group effect after sex/CMV adjustment; use a ridge logistic model with alpha in {0.1,1,10} selected by inner donor-blocked fourfold CV. The comparator uses top five raw age-group effect assays without stability constraint. The perturbation-invariant candidate is selected among training-only assays that also meet: paired day-0/day-7 within-donor Spearman rho >=0.6 across eligible training donors AND median absolute within-person change at day 7 <= half the absolute baseline young-vs-older median gap. Pairing and stability filters are repeated independently in each outer training fold; no held-out donor Day 7 or Year 2 values may tune selection. If fewer than two eligible assays remain in any fold, the gate fails and no sparse score is claimed. Null: 1,000 donor-level age-label permutations, stratified by sex and CMV jointly where every stratum has both classes (otherwise sex-only), running the same nested selection procedure; use seed 20260929. Primary internal metric: donor-held-out AUROC at baseline, and Day-7 AUROC from the SAME frozen fold model. Perturbation retention is Day-7 AUROC minus Day-0 AUROC, with donor bootstrap CI over 2,000 resamples (seed 20260930). Correct confidence intervals for paired donors. Unstable comparator and full-assay model are descriptive benchmarks, not separate confirmatory discoveries.

## External test and transfer limits

Only after full development is frozen, refit a final five-assay model on all 92 Sound Life baseline donors with the same predeclared filters. Require age-group internal AUROC >=0.70 and day-7 AUROC drop no worse than 0.10 point in held-out evaluation, with donor-label permutation p<0.05, **before** opening the 229-donor older-cohort NPX outcomes. External cohort consists of one `Immune Variation Day 0` sample per donor, age 40-89+ (9 subjects have 89+ and cannot be modeled as exact age); use only exact assay IDs common to platforms. **The binary young-vs-older target has almost no young adult counterpart in this second cohort.** External evaluation is therefore NOT the same classifier AUROC or a claim of calibrated ages: freeze the model's continuous score and test its monotonic Spearman association with external donor age among numeric ages 40-89 using a prespecified partial rank regression adjusted for sex, CMV and Olink batch. Require positive slope p<0.05 and signs of at least four of five assay-wise age correlations consistent with development, after BH correction over the five assays. All external normalization uses Sound Life training estimates, and batch shifts/LOD may invalidate score transfer. If the cross-platform NPX scale differs enough that no stable anchors exist, report no-go; do not re-center on external outcomes to recover a positive. Nine 89+ donors excluded from numeric-age correlation and analyzed descriptively as a censored top-age group.

## Stop/claim rules

A passing result can say: a small, preselected assay set was associated with age group in held-out Sound Life donors and was stable over acute vaccination at the stated threshold; the frozen score had a directionally consistent association with chronological age in the older cohort. It cannot say new rejuvenation biology, individual biological age, lifespan prediction, clinical utility, causal mechanism, independent platform calibration, or novelty simply from a small score. A failed eligibility, stability, internal AUROC, transfer or adjusted association gate must be reported. Any code bug repair is logged with frozen inputs and outputs preserved. No figures, paper or user-facing claim before results and audit are real.
