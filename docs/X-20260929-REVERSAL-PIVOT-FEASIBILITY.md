# Reversal-signature pivot: source and novelty feasibility ledger

Screened 2026-09-29, 07:25 IST. This is a source audit, not a computed result, a new preregistration or a claim of discovery. Earlier A/B negatives remain unchanged. Parent capacity rule: no heavy downloads or alignments without a separate plan and green-light.

## The precise proposed claim

A formal transcriptome-wide test might ask whether the reversed-polyp state restores an ordinary colonial-polyp developmental expression program more than a generic injury/stress program, with donor-level uncertainty and an independent validation cohort. A mere comparison of normal and reversed polyps is **not novel**: Matsumoto/Miglietta's 2025 preprint already constructed super-transcripts from colonial polyp, medusa, cyst and reversed-polyp RNA, averaged normalized expression per stage, and reported normal-to-reversed differences for named SIRT, telomere, HSP and developmental-factor candidates. No new finding has been computed here. Primary text: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.07.02.660568v1.full-text (Methods and Results).

## Feasibility call and future locked design

**No-go for a new positive or an inferential test now.** The basic four-stage comparison is prior art; training and proposed holdout are not clearly independent matched contrasts; per-run specimen identity and a downloadable full count matrix remain unverified. The 35.9 GB nine-run raw requirement exceeds the current 1.9 GB free disk. These are evidence limits, not reasons to choose a positive by changing the score.

If the missing primary records become accessible and capacity is allocated, first make a new dated lock before opening expression outcomes. Freeze (i) the ordinary-polyp versus reversed-polyp donor crosswalk, tissue/collection/prep comparability and independent holdout; (ii) gene universe and cross-study ortholog mapping; (iii) a developmental-restoration similarity statistic against prespecified polyp/medusa signatures; (iv) a competing stress-response score, expression/length-matched nulls, donor-label permutations, and FDR family; (v) a decision rule that yields a negative when the effect is not reproducible. Avoid interpreting RNA similarity as cell lineage or causal rejuvenation. If these prerequisites fail, retire this jellyfish pivot and prefer a richer independent public dataset, such as an appropriately donor-resolved NASA OSDR study, under its own project/lock rather than blending it into 26b.

Next bounded unblocker: obtain an authoritative sample-to-run/extraction crosswalk and verify whether the 2025 Appendix contains complete per-run counts. Do **not** download 35.9 GB or run alignment under this ledger. No ETA for inferential results can be honest until those facts and compute capacity are settled.
