## A. Panel-free gene-family discovery

Universe: predicted proteins from the public T. dohrnii main assembly and an independently sourced, annotated comparator genome. Do not filter either proteome by human longevity/GenAge membership before family construction. Source/version/annotation quality and orthology coverage must be recorded. Cluster all-vs-all proteins into families at a fixed similarity/coverage criterion declared in executable configuration before ranking; record sensitivity to at least one stricter criterion. Normalize family sizes for proteome completeness and annotation fragmentation; represent uncertain gene models explicitly. Freeze ranking on T. dohrnii main vs comparator before inspecting a hold-out species/independent T. dohrnii assembly. Validation gate: top-ranked family expansions must be supported by distinct intact loci in an independent assembly or unique-flank long-read evidence, with held-out-species comparison and a multiplicity-controlled empirical null over all tested families. Aging-pathway overlap is a *post-ranking* annotation, not the training label. A top family failing structural or held-out validation is negative. If no suitable whole-genome protein predictions/comparator and independent validation exist, report source gap and do not relabel the existing GenAge list scan as panel-free.
